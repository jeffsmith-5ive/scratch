## 🚀 Key Subsystems

1. **Craft Beer & Merch Storefront**: Caribbean craft brew showcase, regional delivery (Trinidad & Tobago) and taproom collection.
2. **Craft Brew Lab ("Craft Your Own")**: Custom recipe builder with real-time ABV/IBU mathematical calculation and batch fermentation tracking.
3. **Krewe Loyalty Program**: 4-Tier gamification (*Road Crew*, *Mas Band*, *Section Leader*, *Inner Circle*) with dynamic points and achievement badges.
4. **Brewer Guide AI ("LimeBot")**: Natural language conversational sommelier for craft beer recommendations, pairing advice, and order assistance.
5. **Admin Operations Center**: Executive KPI monitoring, order dispatching, brewing queue management, and inventory replenishment alerts.
